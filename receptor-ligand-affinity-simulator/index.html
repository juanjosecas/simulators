Model limits

This is a reversible 1:1 binding model with one binding site per receptor and no cooperativity.

It represents molecular binding only. It does not include signaling efficacy, receptor activation, allostery, internalization or multiple receptor states.

The time course starts from free A₀ and R₀ with no preformed AR complex.

A + R ⇌ AR, Kd = k₂/k₁, KA = k₁/k₂
